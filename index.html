My dear Salma,

I know I forgot Valentine’s Day. I know I was supposed to have a gift ready, plan something sweet, and make you feel extra special. Instead, my memory apparently decided to take the day off. Not its finest moment. 😅

So consider this website my tiny apology, and a promise that the real surprise is still coming. I’ll make it up to you — and next time, I’m setting seventeen reminders. 😂

With all my love (and a very guilty face),
